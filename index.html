OUR STORY

More than a meal.

RAYYOKA is a food experience created to connect people through familiar favourites, bold flavours and thoughtful hospitality. From momo and chawmin to fried rice, noodles and drinks, our goal is simple: make every order feel worth remembering.

Our identity brings together Himalayan inspiration, warmth and a modern food-business spirit — designed to grow from a local idea into a meaningful brand.

“Flavors that connect — people, places, memories and moments.”
THE RAYYOKA SPIRIT
