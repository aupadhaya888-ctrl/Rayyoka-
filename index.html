OUR STORY

More than a meal.

RAYYOKA is a food experience built around taste, imagination, care, focus and blessings. Our menu brings together momo, chawmin, fried rice, noodles and drinks in a simple, welcoming experience.

We are building a distinct food brand with Himalayan inspiration and a modern spirit — one memorable order at a time.
