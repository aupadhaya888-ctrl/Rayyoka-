OUR STORY

More than a meal.

RAYYOKA is a food experience created to connect people through familiar favourites, thoughtful hospitality and memorable flavour.

Our first menu focuses on momo, chawmin, fried rice, noodles and drinks — simple food made with purpose, consistency and heart.
