ABOUT RAYYOKA

More than food. A connection.

RAYYOKA is built around a simple belief: good food can connect people, places, memories and moments.

Our food story brings together Himalayan inspiration, modern presentation and the warmth of hospitality. We focus on favourites people already love while creating a brand with its own identity and purpose.

“Flavors that connect — people, places, memories and moments.”
