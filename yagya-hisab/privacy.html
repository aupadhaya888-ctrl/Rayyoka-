YagyaHisab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

YagyaHisab is a Nepali and English financial calculator. This draft policy describes the intended service. Review and update it before enabling advertising or payments.

Information we process

  • Account information such as your email address, handled by Supabase Authentication.
  • Saved calculations and budget entries, if you choose to use those features. These may contain financial information and are private to your account under database access rules.
  • Payment order identifiers and payment status needed to verify a Premium purchase. We do not ask for or store your eSewa password, PIN or OTP.
  • Limited technical information handled by hosting, security, and (once enabled) advertising/payment providers.

Calculator inputs

Basic calculations run in your browser. If you save a calculation or budget, the information is sent to Supabase so it can be associated with your account. Do not save details that you do not want stored online.

Advertising

Advertising is not enabled yet. If advertising is introduced, this policy must be updated with the provider, cookie/device identifiers, purposes, consent controls where required, and instructions for managing ad preferences before ads are shown.

Payments

Premium is planned at NPR 99 for 30 days, with manual renewal initially. Payment will remain disabled until merchant approval and secure server-side verification are configured. eSewa processes payment credentials on its own service; YagyaHisab should receive only transaction and verification details.

Retention and security

We retain account data while your account is active and as needed for support, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations. We use account-based database access controls, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop using the app, avoid saving calculations, request account/data assistance, and choose not to purchase Premium. Contact the app owner through the support channel published with the app. Add a real support email before public launch.

Children and changes

YagyaHisab is a general-purpose finance tool and is not designed to collect children's personal data. We may update this policy and will revise the effective date when material changes are made.
